# ☕ Sueño Coffee

Official digital customer experience and loyalty platform for **Sueño Coffee**.

## Vision

Turn one-time coffee buyers into regular customers by giving customers a simple digital experience while giving the business better visibility into customer behavior.

## Customer Experience

Customers will eventually be able to:

- Create an account
- View the Sueño Coffee menu
- Join the digital loyalty program
- Earn points or stamps
- Track rewards
- Redeem rewards
- View promotions
- See their activity
- Access Sueño through QR codes

## Admin Experience

Sueño management will eventually be able to:

- Manage customers
- Manage loyalty points and rewards
- Create promotions
- Manage the menu
- View new vs returning customers
- Monitor customer activity
- View basic analytics
- Identify popular products and slower periods
